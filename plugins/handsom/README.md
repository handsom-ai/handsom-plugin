# Handsom

Run a Handsom store from your own agent. Handsom handles a Shopify store's support tickets, and this plugin lets you work its queue and apply a brand's policies, documents and interview notes to its rules, facts and knowledge.

## Use it

Install the plugin, connect the Handsom connector and sign in. Then ask your agent what needs you, or give it a brand's documents and ask it to apply them to the store.

## Data

The plugin connects to your Handsom account through https://api.handsom.ai/mcp. It works as you, on the stores you can reach, and can do what you can there. That includes reading customer conversations, which reach your agent app when you ask it to.
